Example valley - flood zoning and evacuation

Report tables – 31 October 2026

Key figures

  • The flood zones (Current situation) cover 911.9 ha in total: Red 473.6 ha, Orange 311.2 ha, Yellow 127.1 ha.
  • The river protection strip covers 53.0 ha.
  • 59.1 ha of existing settlements lie in the red or orange zones.
  • Compared with "Current situation", the flood zones of "Raised banks" are 190.3 ha (21%) smaller.
  • 6 settlements in 5 evacuation zones need to be evacuated (111.1 ha).
  • 5 safe havens were found (5 of 5 evacuation zones); each is about the same size as its zone.
  • 6 evacuation routes, 3.8 km in total. The longest is V-03-R1: 0.9 km, about 18 minutes on foot.
  • 37% of the total route length is on roads and tracks.
  • 5 routes cross ground under deep water (0.3 m or more by default) in the design flood, 938 m in total. People there must leave before the flood arrives; check these stretches on site.

Terms used

  • Design event: the largest flood used for planning (e.g. the 100-year flood). Shallow water below the minimum depth is ignored.
  • Evacuation zone: one or more settlements of the same type, close to each other, that are evacuated together. Codes are made of the type initials and a number.
  • Safe haven: dry, open ground outside the flood zones where people gather and stay during a flood. "Above flood level?" says whether the ground was checked against the nearby flood water level with a terrain model (DEM).
  • Evacuation route: the easiest walking route from the sector to the safe haven. Roads are preferred, then footpaths, then open ground; deep water and steep slopes are avoided and the river is crossed only on roads.
  • Walking time: at about 3.1 km/h, the pace of people walking with children and belongings.
  • Through flood water: length of the route where the design flood is deep enough to be unsafe (0.3 m or more by default). It is usually unavoidable for settlements inside the floodplain, so these places must be evacuated early, before the water rises.
