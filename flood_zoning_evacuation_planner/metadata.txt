about=Flood Zoning & Evacuation Planner adds Processing tools that turn flood-depth rasters from any hydraulic model into practical flood-risk management layers, for any area in the world.

    Tools in this version:
    - River corridor: river channel polygon and protection buffer, from river polygons, river lines, or the channel visible in a flood-depth raster.
    - Spatial planning zones: no-development (red), restricted (orange) and conditional (yellow) development zones and a river protection zone (green), based on flood probability and depth, with a rule for existing settlements and smoothing into a few continuous zones.

    All thresholds are adjustable. Results are styled automatically. Works in any projected coordinate system; layers in geographic coordinates are processed in the local UTM zone.

    No extra Python packages are needed: the tools use only what is shipped with QGIS (GDAL and NumPy).
